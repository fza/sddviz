One bar, one row of filters, and two views a reader switches between, with a panel beside them.

A bar across the top names the open repository and the revision its graph reads at, and carries the
control letting go of it. It names nothing while nothing is open. `Repository` still carries a
`Name`, which is the directory the graph lives in. The back end still splits it, because a path
belongs to the front end to show and never to take apart. A segmented control below it
chooses the view. That control appears only once a graph opens, because a strip offering one dead
choice tells a reader nothing. A control opening a repository sits centred in the area until then.

Entries is where a reader lands. A list of what a graph holds reads straight away, where a drawn
graph asks for exploring first.

## What the row above the views carries

The search field, the topic filter, and a control dropping the done signals sit above both views and
narrow what each draws. The graph shows what a search and its filters left standing rather than the
whole graph every time. Every one of those choices outlives a switch between the views.

## What the graph draws

Everything the earlier record fixed about the drawing still holds, and only its place in the window
changes. An entry is one shape, coloured by whether it decides or signals, greyed where the graph
retired it, and sized by how many links reach it. A link is one line, coloured by its kind, pointing
at what it names. A halo marks distance from the selection: red on the selection, purple and smaller
one link away, and purple and smaller again two links away. The camera flies to the selection.
Repulsion reaches a bounded distance, so a group linking to nothing else stays beside the body it
belongs to. The graph takes the whole area under the row of filters, to the window's own edges.

One selection still serves every part. Choosing in one place shows in the others, and the table
scrolls to a row the graph named. The two views no longer sit side by side: a reader switches between
them, and the panel stays beside whichever one is open. A control on each row of the table draws that
entry in the graph.

## What the table does

Every column sorts except the counter and the summary, and a search sorts the same way with its score
column among the rest. Clearing the sort brings back the order sdd ranked. A width fixes each column
and a pull on its edge changes it, the summary alone taking what the rest leave. Rows draw only where
the reader is looking, which is what answers a sort over a thousand entries inside a frame.

## What the panel does

The panel carries everything the earlier record fixed. It shows the entry's type, layer, kind,
intent, and derived status, its summary, its topics with the area marked, its participants, and its
confidence. Below those sit the links it declares, the links reaching it, and the identifiers it
names that the graph does not hold. Each link selects the entry at its other end.

Two things change. The body renders as the Markdown it is, rather than as blocks of rejoined prose.
A pull on the panel's own edge widens it, between the width its headings need and the width that
would crowd out the view beside it.

## What it costs

A search holds its ranking only until a reader picks a heading, and sorting replaces it until they
clear the sort. The graph redraws when a filter changes, which throws away a settled layout. None of
this reaches a reader with no repository open, so a server build ejecting one strands them, since
choosing a directory needs a window system.

Nothing follows from this on its own, which is what makes it terminal at capture.
